## Build notes for future sessions

- Do not force the new site to mirror the current site's thin homepage.
- Pull the strongest ministry content into the homepage even if it currently lives deeper in the sitemap.
- Reuse the existing template's strongest cards and sections instead of redesigning everything from scratch.
- Favor clarity and warmth over overly clever copy.
